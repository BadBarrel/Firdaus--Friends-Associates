## Game modes

- **🏝️ Island**: build, collect gold, grow food, hatch, breed and feed.
- **🗺️ Adventure** (Lv 1): 40 stages across 🌋 Ember Coast, 🌲 Whispering Woods, 🏔️ Frozen Peaks, 🌌 Shadow Rift. Every 10th stage is a boss. Up to 3 stars per stage; gems on first clear.
- **🏟️ Arena** (Lv 4): fight rival riders (Easy / Normal / Hard) for trophies and climb 🥉 Bronze → 🥈 Silver → 🥇 Gold → 🏆 Platinum → 💠 Diamond → 👑 Dragonlord. Promotions give gems.
- **🗼 Tower of Trials** (Lv 8): endless floors that keep getting harder, with gems every 5 floors.
- **📜 Quests**: daily login streak, 3 daily quests, achievements.

Battles are turn-based, with teams of up to 3 dragons fighting one at a time.

## Gems (earned only by playing)

Level up (+3), daily login streak, daily quests (+5 each, +10 for all), achievements, Adventure first clears, Arena promotions, Tower every 5 floors. Spend them to finish timers or swap for gold/food.

## Settings

- **Game speed**: Normal, Fast (5×), Turbo (30×) or Instant for breeding, hatching and crops.
- **Backup**: copy a backup code and restore it later. Your save stays on your phone.
